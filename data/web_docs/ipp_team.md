Our authors and advisors have been selected to highlight the problems of the present day, solutions that can make a free-market Iran a prosperous state in an interim period, and propose long-term solutions. Fulfilling roles with senior economic advisors for major DC-based think tanks, to serving as principal economists on the Federal Reserve Board, to academia at several universities across the country, hospital systems, the authors and advisors who have begun to shape this plan are some of our community’s very best.

Our mission is to lay out the path that can lead to a free, fair, and prosperous Iran and to show that the dreams millions have for a new Iran are not only attainable, but measurable.
